How to update

Choose the method that works best for you.

01

Update inside the app

Open Assignment Tracker and tap 🔄 Check for Updates. If a compatible OTA update is available, you can choose to install it.

02

Download the latest APK

Download the latest APK from this website and install it on your Android device. Back up your assignments first, and do not uninstall your existing app if you want to preserve locally stored data.
